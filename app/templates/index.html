Churn Predictor

Telco customer retention model

What this does

Enter a customer's account profile and the model estimates whether they are likely to cancel their service in the near term. Built on the public Telco Customer Churn dataset (7,043 customers, 19 features).

How a prediction is made

  1. The form is sent as JSON to the /predict endpoint.
  2. The server encodes categorical fields and scales numeric ones with the same pipeline used in training.
  3. The trained classifier returns a churn label and, if available, a probability.

Signals the model weighs most

ContractMonth-to-month is the strongest churn signal
TenureRisk drops sharply after the first year
InternetFiber optic customers churn more often
PaymentElectronic check correlates with churn
